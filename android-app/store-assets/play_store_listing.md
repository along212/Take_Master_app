## Full Description (2,063 / 4,000 chars max)
```text
Recorded the same performance a few times and nailed different sections in each take? Take Master brings your best moments together into one seamless final video — 100% offline on your Android device.

Designed for musicians, singers, instrumentalists, actors, creators, and multi-camera shoots, Take Master automatically aligns up to 6 video takes by audio down to sub-millisecond precision so you can audition angles and stitch cuts without awkward silences or timing glitches.

KEY FEATURES
• Sub-Millisecond Auto Audio Alignment
  Automatically analyzes each take's audio (with transient suppression for drums, instruments, and vocals) and lines up all takes vertically on one unified Master Timeline — plus ±1 ms / ±5 ms / ±20 ms manual fine nudge when needed.

• Comp Mode & Multi-Take Compare
  - Comp Mode: Watch and listen to your edited Master Comp exactly as it will export, with live equal-power audio crossfades across cuts.
  - Compare Mode (Mute & Solo): Mix multiple unmuted takes simultaneously along the timeline to check sync accuracy and compare performances.
  - Multi-Angle Split View: Watch up to 4 synchronized camera angles side by side and tap any tile to audition or focus.

• Fast Mobile Timeline Editing
  - Pan cleanly across zoomed waveforms (1x–12x).
  - Swipe Comp directly across any take lane to assign sections, or tap to switch takes.
  - Split Tap: Tap any take lane to split directly into that take, or tap the Master track to cycle through takes.
  - Drag white cut handles to move cut points, yellow handles to trim start/end, or use Auto-Split and up to 25-step Undo.

• Gapless MP4 Export to Gallery
  - Active Take Audio with configurable equal-power crossfades (2–50 ms) and optional quiet-valley transient snapping.
  - Or Lock Audio Track to keep one continuous audio performance while cutting between camera angles.
  - Export in Original Resolution or fast 1080p HD straight to Movies/TakeMaster.

• 100% Local & Private
  Everything runs offline on your phone — no accounts, no uploads, no watermarks, and no ads.
```
