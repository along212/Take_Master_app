Take Master — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

Take Master is an offline multi-take audio alignment and video comping studio for Android. Your privacy is fully respected: the App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

1. Data Collection & On-Device Processing

  • No Personal Data Collected: Take Master requires no account or login and contains no analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs.
  • 100% Local Media Processing: All video decoding, audio alignment, multi-take previewing, and MP4 stitching happen locally on your device. Your media files never leave your phone unless you explicitly share an exported video yourself.
  • Local Temporary Cache: Temporary decoded audio cache files are stored strictly inside Android's private app cache directory during editing and cleaned up automatically.

2. Permissions & File Access

  • Android Photo Picker: Accesses only the specific video files you choose to import.
  • Gallery Export (MediaStore): Writes finished exports to Movies/TakeMaster on your device.
  • Zero Background Tracking: No location, contacts, microphone, camera, or network tracking permissions are used.

3. Third-Party Services & Ads

Take Master displays no ads and sends no data to third parties.

4. Children's Privacy

No personal information is collected from any users, regardless of age.

5. Contact

For privacy questions or support, please reach out via the project repository: github.com/along212/Take_Master_app/issues
