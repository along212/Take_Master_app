# Privacy Policy for Take Master

**Effective Date:** November 20, 2026

**Take Master** ("the App") is built for local, offline multi-take video and audio editing on your Android device. Your privacy is fully respected: the App does **not** collect, store on external servers, sell, or share any personal data.

## 1. Data Collection and Processing
- **No Personal Data Collected:** Take Master does not require an account, login, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or cloud service.
- **100% On-Device Media Processing:** All video loading, audio extraction, alignment, preview playback, and MP4 stitching/exporting happen strictly offline in memory and temporary app cache on your phone. Your videos and audio never leave your device unless you explicitly choose to share an exported file using Android's system Share sheet.
- **Temporary Cache Files:** During editing, decoded temporary PCM audio files and intermediate export files may be cached locally in Android's private app cache directory and cleaned up automatically.

## 2. Permissions & Media Access
- **Android Photo Picker:** Video clips are accessed solely through the system Android Photo Picker for the specific videos you select.
- **Gallery Export (`MediaStore`):** Finished videos are saved to your device's `Movies/TakeMaster` folder via Android's `MediaStore` API.
- **No Background Tracking:** Take Master does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, or network tracking permissions.

## 3. Third-Party Services & Ads
- Take Master contains **no advertisements** and integrates **no third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking services**.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Because Take Master collects no data from any users, no personal information from children or adults is ever collected or stored remotely.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
If future versions introduce any optional network features, this Privacy Policy will be updated at the hosted repository URL before release.
